Fly legal in Canada

No licence. No registration. Just fly smart.

Because the Mini 5 Pro weighs under 250 g, Transport Canada doesn't require a drone licence or registration to fly it. A few common-sense rules still apply:

  • Stay under 122 m (400 ft) and keep the drone where you can see it.
  • Keep well away from airports, heliports, and any emergency scenes.
  • Don't fly over crowds — and advertised public events need a special permit.
  • Skip city parks: Toronto bylaws prohibit drones in city parks, and national/provincial parks ban them too. Private property (with permission), open rural areas and many spots outside the city are fair game.
  • Respect privacy — don't hover over neighbours or film people without permission.

We go over all of this in 5 minutes at pickup and point you to great legal flying spots near Toronto. Note: accessories that push take-off weight to 250 g+ change the rules — we'll keep you under. Renters are responsible for flying legally and safely (see rental agreement).

FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Do I need a drone licence to rent it?
No. The Mini 5 Pro weighs 249 g, which is under Transport Canada's 250 g threshold — no licence and no registration needed. You just need to fly responsibly (we'll show you how at pickup).
I've never flown a drone. Can I still rent?
Yes! The Mini 5 Pro has obstacle sensing in every direction, auto take-off/landing and a return-to-home button. Every rental includes a 10-minute hands-on intro, and we're a text away if you get stuck.
How do the deposit and payment work?
Rental payment is by Interac e-Transfer or cash at pickup. The $500 deposit is a refundable hold — e-Transfer (returned on drop-off) or a card pre-authorization that's simply released. We also check photo ID and sign a one-page rental agreement.
What if the drone gets damaged or lost?
Minor wear is on us. Crashes or damage come out of the deposit at fair repair cost — never more than the deposit for damage. If the drone is lost or flown away, the deposit is kept and the rental agreement covers the difference. Fly carefully and this never comes up.
Where do we meet for pickup?
We're in North York, Toronto — easy to reach by TTC or car. Exact meetup spot is confirmed when we confirm your booking. Delivery within the GTA can be arranged for a small fee.
What happens if the weather is bad?
Drones and rain don't mix. If the forecast is bad for your rental or shoot day, we reschedule for free — no questions asked.
Can you fly for my business or listing anywhere in the GTA?
Mostly yes — private properties, listings, and businesses across the GTA are usually straightforward. Some locations (near airports, over crowds, advertised events, city parks) have restrictions. Send us the address and we'll confirm quickly.
How do I get my photos and videos after a shoot?
Through a private download link (full resolution, no watermarks) within 48 hours — or 24 hours with rush delivery. You get full usage rights for your marketing and socials.
